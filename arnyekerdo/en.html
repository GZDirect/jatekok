Chapter 1 · Stealth mission

Shinobi

Night in the Japanese mountains. Because of a false warrant, the police have sealed off the valley where you are hiding: 6 officers comb the forest with flashlights and pistols. Get from the southern end to the northern road – as a shadow, or by taking them out one by one.

Controls and tools
Left thumb
move (computer: W A S D)
Right thumb
look around (computer: click the game area, then look with the mouse; Esc releases it)
Crouch
you are quiet, and invisible in a bush (right mouse button or C)
Run
fast but loud: push the joystick forward to the RUN arc (computer: Q or Shift)
Tanto, tekko
one blow from behind: the tanto kills, the tekko only knocks out (click or E)
Grapnel
aim at a giant tree with dense foliage: when it glows faintly, you can climb up
Claws
climb a giant tree's trunk (1–8: switch tools)
In the tree
they only see you if they shine a light on you; drop down to attack from above if you hold the tanto or tekko (space)
Pebble
its clatter lures nearby officers (5)
Body
drag a downed officer into a bush where they won't find him (Drag button, G)
Caltrops
throw them ahead, or scatter them behind you while running; whoever steps on them stops (6)
Shuriken
throwing star: wounds; if it misses, its clatter lures, and you can pick it up (8)
Blowgun
sleep dart: whoever you hit falls asleep (7)
Smoke bomb
blinds nearby officers and hides you (9 or F)
Jump
Jump button or space: hop over logs, rocks and crates; it also drops you from a tree
? and !
above an officer's head: the question mark fills with suspicion, the red exclamation mark means alarm
